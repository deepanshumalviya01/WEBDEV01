Research Highlights & Laboratories

Discover our cutting-edge research initiatives that are shaping the future of technology.

Centre for AI & Cognitive Robotics

High-end GPU-powered research workstations executing deep vision pipelines, autonomous models, and LLM fine-tuning supported by university innovation grants.

Cyber Security & Digital Forensics Lab

A closed-circuit hardware network dedicated to live malware testing, penetration defense mechanisms, and secure packet inspection simulations.

IoT & Embedded Systems Workshop

Equipped with wireless sensor transceivers, microcontrollers, and cloud-bridge gateways for smart agriculture and automated campus metering projects.

Big Data & Cloud Sandbox

Collaborative multi-node cluster providing students hands-on environment for distributed databases, streaming ETL frameworks, and Apache ecosystem tools.
